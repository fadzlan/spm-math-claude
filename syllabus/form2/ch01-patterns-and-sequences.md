# Form 2, Chapter 1: Patterns and Sequences

## Overview
Develops pattern recognition: number patterns, sequences, terms, and rules of sequences. Core work identifies, describes, completes and extends patterns; finds specified terms; generalises through numbers, words and algebraic expressions; and solves contextual sequence problems. A finite list can fit many different rules, so every generated item must state or imply the intended rule family rather than claiming that its rule is uniquely discoverable. Quadratic and interleaved patterns are enrichment that can preview later algebraic reasoning, not replacements for the Form 2 core progression.

## Prerequisites
Integer/fraction operations; algebraic expressions (Form 1 Ch5).

## Difficulty strategies

### 1.1 Patterns
- **Easy**: +/− constant difference, small numbers (3, 7, 11, 15…).
- **Medium**: multiplicative patterns (×2, ×3), square/cube patterns, Fibonacci-type (add previous two).
- **Hard (core)**: patterns of figures where the rule must be described in words and then used to extend or find a specified figure; include a clear construction rule or enough structural information to remove unintended ambiguity.
- **Enrichment**: alternating or two-rule interleaved patterns (3, 5, 8, 10, 13, 15…) and constant-second-difference patterns (2, 5, 10, 17, 26, with the intended rule Tₙ = n² + 1).

### 1.2 Sequences
- **Easy**: find next term / 5th term by continuing rule.
- **Medium**: find T₁₀ or T₁₅ without listing (spot rule); determine if a given number is a term (solve and check integer).
- **Hard (core)**: negative or decimal difference sequences; determine *which term number* equals a value and verify that the index is a positive integer.
- **Enrichment**: mixed/interleaved sequences, with the two position-based rules stated or strongly signposted.

### 1.3 nth term & applications
- **Easy**: nth term from arithmetic sequence with small difference (Tₙ = 4n + 1).
- **Medium**: derive Tₙ from any arithmetic sequence including decreasing ones (Tₙ = 20 − 3n); verify by computing a term.
- **Hard (core)**: matchstick/seating patterns requiring formula derivation from a drawn pattern (diagram needed); contextual arithmetic growth, with wording that distinguishes the amount in week k from the cumulative total saved.
- **Enrichment / cross-topic**: problems asking for the first term exceeding a threshold by solving Tₙ > k; tag with the Form 1 inequalities prerequisite and do not use them as the default hard pattern task.
- Distractors: off-by-one in nth term (using a instead of a(n−1) logic errors); sign errors for decreasing sequences; assuming all patterns are arithmetic.

## Visual aids
- **RECOMMENDED** for matchstick/shape pattern questions — SVG drawings of the first 3–4 figures of the pattern (grids of dots, triangles of matchsticks, growing squares).
- Pure number sequences: **NONE**.

## Generator notes
- Required item metadata: `scope: core|enrichment`, `rule_family` (for example `arithmetic`, `geometric`, `recursive_previous_two`, `figural_linear`, `quadratic` or `interleaved`), `rule_representation: numbers|words|algebraic|multiple`, and `intended_rule`. Use `prerequisite_links` for cross-topic methods such as inequalities.
- A finite prefix does not determine a unique continuation. Either state the rule, name/signpost its family, give a generative diagram/context, or accept every continuation that follows a clearly explained valid rule. Do not mark a guessed continuation as uniquely correct from an underspecified prefix.
- Preserve the core order: identify/describe → complete/extend → find a specified term → generalise in numbers, words and algebra → solve a contextual problem. Enrichment must not be needed to demonstrate core mastery.
- For "is k a term?" questions, choose k so that it sometimes is and sometimes isn't; if not, (k − b)/a must be non-integer or non-positive.
- Matchstick patterns: linear-growth patterns (add a constant per figure) suit core items. Two-dimensional growth such as square arrays uses quadratic counting and must be tagged enrichment/Form 4 preview.
- Validate the keyed rule against every displayed term or figure, and independently compute the requested term/index. For figural patterns, derive the drawing and count from the same internal model.
- Cross-link: algebraic skills from Form 1 Ch5/6; inequality-threshold enrichment links to Form 1 Ch7; quadratic-pattern enrichment previews Form 4 Ch1.
