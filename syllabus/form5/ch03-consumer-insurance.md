# Form 5, Chapter 3: Consumer Mathematics: Insurance

## Overview
Insurance concepts and computations: risk and risk transfer; the purpose and non-profit/indemnity principle of insurance; life and general insurance (including medical, motor, fire, personal accident and travel); policy components (premium, sum insured, deductible and co-insurance); premium factors; and compensation under stated policy rules. Motor no-claim discount (NCD) may be used as a self-contained rate-table context, but is not assumed core knowledge.

## Prerequisites
Form 3 Ch3 (percentages, financial arithmetic); Form 4 Ch10 (financial planning).

## Difficulty strategies

### 3.1
- **Easy**: match insurance type to scenario (medical card for surgery).
- **Medium**: interpret a policy schedule table (premium, coverage items and limits); explain the deductible's effect under the policy's stated application rule.
- **Hard**: recommend between two policies weighing premium, deductible, coverage, exclusions and limits — justify quantitatively while respecting the indemnity/non-profit principle.

### 3.2
- **Easy**: premium = supplied rate × sum/1000; simple deductible payout when the item states that the deductible is subtracted from the covered loss (loss RM8 000, deductible RM500 ⇒ RM7 500, subject to the stated policy limit).
- **Medium**: NCD applied using a schedule printed in the item (for example, base premium RM1 200 and a supplied 45% rate ⇒ RM660); payout capped by the applicable covered-loss and policy limits; loss below the deductible (payout RM0 under a stated excess rule).
- **Hard**: co-insurance problems in which the item states the required-insurance percentage, proportional-settlement rule, deductible order and caps. For example, if a stated policy applies proportional settlement first and then subtracts a RM1 000 deductible, a property worth RM500 000, insured for RM300 000 with an 80% requirement and RM100 000 covered loss gives RM100 000×(RM300 000/RM400 000)−RM1 000=RM74 000 before applying the stated caps; if every applicable limit is at least RM74 000, the payout is RM74 000. Other hard items may find loss given payout (reverse), compare total cost over a period (premium + uncompensated covered loss), or cover multiple incidents only when per-incident deductibles and aggregate limits are explicit; NCD changes over years use a supplied dated ladder with claim/reset rules.
- Distractors: ignoring the stated deductible order; applying the co-insurance ratio to premium instead of loss; using property value instead of stated required insurance; applying NCD to sum insured rather than the premium base specified by the schedule; allowing payout to exceed the covered loss or an applicable policy limit.

## Visual aids
- **RECOMMENDED** — policy schedule/quotation tables (the natural representation); premium rate tables (per RM1000); dated NCD ladder tables when NCD is assessed. Charts not needed.

## Generator notes
- Use plausible Malaysian figures, but state all values rather than treating them as current market rules. Co-insurance percentages, deductibles and limits are item data.
- Present premium rate tables clearly (for example, RM x per RM1 000 sum insured, by plan), including the premium basis, rounding rule and all relevant fees or discounts.
- Every NCD item must reproduce a complete schedule sufficient to solve it, label the jurisdiction/product context, include an `as_of` date, and state how claims affect the discount. Never rely on a memorised NCD scale or imply that an illustrative rate is current.
- Treat compensation as policy-specific. State: the covered loss; required-insurance amount or how to calculate it; whether proportional settlement applies and how its ratio is capped; whether the deductible is applied before or after that adjustment; and each per-incident, category, aggregate and sum-insured limit needed by the item.
- Compute payouts in the exact stated order and validate them independently. Enforce payout ≥0 and payout ≤ covered loss after any stated uncovered portion; enforce every applicable sublimit, incident limit, aggregate remaining limit and overall policy/sum-insured limit. Reject generated items whose reverse question has no unique solution under the caps.
- In policy comparisons, use the same scenario and time period for every policy and account for premium, insured payout and the policyholder's uncompensated covered loss without double-counting. Preserve the indemnity/non-profit principle.
- A multi-part format may ask for (a) a premium from a supplied rate/NCD table, (b) payout for an incident with a deductible, and (c) a co-insurance penalty, provided each governing rule is fully stated.
